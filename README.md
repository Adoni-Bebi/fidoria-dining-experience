# Fidoria Dining Experience

Act as a professional website designer, UI/UX expert, and frontend developer. Create a beautiful, modern, premium-quality, fully responsive restaurant website for Fidoria Noodles & Chicken, located in Adama, Oromia, Ethiopia.

REFERENCE WEBSITE:
Use Yegna Gebeta's website (https://www.yegnagebeta.com.et/en) as inspiration for the professional restaurant presentation, attractive food photography, branding, navigation, and menu experience. Do not copy its exact design, text, logo, or proprietary assets. Create an original design specifically for Fidoria Noodles & Chicken.

MAIN OBJECTIVE:
Build a restaurant website that looks like a real, established restaurant business—not a generic template or student project. It should impress the restaurant owner and be suitable for presenting as a professional website proposal.

DESIGN DIRECTION:

Modern, elegant, premium restaurant aesthetic.

Warm colors inspired by delicious food: deep charcoal, creamy white, rich orange, and subtle golden accents.

Large, high-quality food photography.

Beautiful typography with clear visual hierarchy.

Generous spacing, rounded image corners, tasteful shadows, and smooth transitions.

A polished layout that works perfectly on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.

Use subtle animations without making the website slow or distracting.

Make the website visually impressive from the first screen.

BRAND IDENTITY:
Restaurant name: Fidoria Noodles & Chicken.
Location: Adama, Oromia, Ethiopia.
Cuisine focus: Noodles and chicken.
Create a simple, elegant text-based logo treatment for the restaurant.
Do not invent a history, awards, customer testimonials, or claims about the restaurant.

WEBSITE STRUCTURE:

NAVIGATION BAR
Create a clean, modern navigation bar featuring:

Fidoria logo or wordmark.

Home.

Menu.

About Us.

Gallery.

Contact.

A prominent "Explore Our Menu" button.
Keep the navigation responsive, with a mobile hamburger menu.

HERO SECTION
Create a visually stunning homepage hero section featuring:

A large, appetizing photograph of noodles or a chicken dish.

A bold headline such as "Good Food. Great Moments."

A short introduction to Fidoria Noodles & Chicken.

A primary button: "Explore Our Menu".

A secondary button: "Find Us".
Use a sophisticated composition with excellent contrast and readable text.
The first screen should look premium and memorable.

FEATURED MENU
Create a beautiful food menu preview with image cards.
Include placeholder categories such as:

Signature Noodles.

Chicken Dishes.

Rice and Sides.

Drinks.
Each card should include a food image, dish name, short description, and price field.
Use sample dishes only as clearly marked placeholders. Do not present invented menu items or prices as verified restaurant information.
Include a "View Full Menu" button.

ABOUT THE RESTAURANT
Create an elegant section introducing Fidoria Noodles & Chicken.
Use a two-column layout with a food photograph on one side and text on the other.
Keep the wording editable and avoid inventing facts about the restaurant's history, ingredients, or cooking methods.

FOOD GALLERY
Build an attractive, responsive image gallery showcasing noodles, chicken, sides, and drinks.
Use high-quality food images that can be replaced with restaurant-approved photographs.
Add subtle hover effects and maintain consistent image dimensions.

WHY VISIT FIDORIA
Create a visually attractive section highlighting the restaurant's food and dining experience.
Use editable content placeholders for qualities the owner can confirm.
Do not invent customer reviews, ratings, awards, or business achievements.

LOCATION AND CONTACT
Create a contact section with:

Restaurant address in Adama, Ethiopia.

Phone number placeholder.

Opening-hours placeholder.

Google Maps location or directions button.

Social media links.

Optional WhatsApp contact button.
Do not invent phone numbers, exact addresses, opening hours, or social media accounts.
Clearly indicate information that still needs confirmation.

FOOTER
Include:

Restaurant name and logo.

Quick navigation links.

Contact information.

Social media icons.

Copyright notice.
Use a clean, professional design.

FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS:

All navigation links must work.

Menu buttons must navigate to the menu section or menu page.

Contact buttons must work once real contact details are supplied.

Google Maps directions must use the verified restaurant location.

WhatsApp must use the owner's verified business number.

The mobile menu must open and close correctly.

Add subtle, smooth scrolling.

Use responsive layouts for mobile, tablet, and desktop.

Optimize image sizes and loading performance.

Provide accessible text contrast, meaningful image descriptions, and keyboard-friendly navigation.

Avoid broken links, nonfunctional buttons, and unnecessary animations.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS:

Use React with clean, reusable components and modern CSS or Tailwind CSS.

Organize the code into logical sections and components.

Use semantic HTML and accessible design patterns.

Add appropriate page titles and meta descriptions for search engines.

Ensure the website loads quickly and works in modern browsers.

Keep menu items, prices, opening hours, and contact details easy to update.

Use real image URLs or properly configured local assets rather than broken image placeholders.

If using external images, confirm they are suitable for commercial use and provide a clear way to replace them with approved restaurant photography.

IMPORTANT CONTENT RULES:

Treat the website as a demonstration until the restaurant owner approves it.

Clearly identify unverified menu items, prices, photos, and business details.

Do not claim that online ordering, reservations, or payments are available unless those features are actually implemented.

Do not invent customer testimonials or restaurant credentials.

FINAL RESULT:
Deliver a complete, visually polished, working restaurant website that looks professionally designed and is ready to demonstrate to the owner of Fidoria Noodles & Chicken.

Prioritize beautiful visual design, excellent mobile responsiveness, realistic restaurant presentation, and a distinctive brand identity.
